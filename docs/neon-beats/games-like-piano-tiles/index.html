Neon Beats

🎮 Tap/click the three lanes. Keyboard: A S D or arrow keys hit lanes, Space starts, P pauses, M mutes.

Piano Tiles nailed a hypnotic formula: notes slide toward you, you tap them in time, and your inputs become the song. People who search for games like it are usually chasing two feelings at once — the flow of a rhythm game and the specific magic of hearing the melody respond to your own accuracy. What they often run into instead is a wall of app installs, song-pack purchases and ads stitched between tracks. The browser version of the genre should cost neither storage nor patience.

Neon Beats is a three-lane tapping rhythm game where the music is not a pre-recorded track but a chiptune synthesized live in your browser — your hits literally play the melody, and a missed note goes silent while the backing beat carries on. Timing windows grade every tap (±60 ms is Perfect, ±130 ms is Good), chains of 20 and 50 hits double and triple your score, and nothing ever fails the run out from under you: the song always finishes, and accuracy decides your S/A/B/C grade.

The daily hook matches the genre's habit loop: Track of the Day generates one new song from the UTC date seed — the same song, the same notes, for every player worldwide — with accuracy ranks from Bronze to Legend and a streak to maintain. No downloads of songs or apps, no account, no ads inside the game; everything runs in one instantly loading page that speaks English and Chinese. Practice mode serves fresh random seeds whenever the daily is done.

Frequently asked questions

What games are like Piano Tiles?

Neon Beats is the same tap-the-notes formula in a browser: notes fall in three lanes, you tap in time, and accurate hits sound the melody itself. It is a free, no-download match for the loop — with the twist that every daily track is brand new and identical worldwide.

Do I need to download songs or the game?

No. The music is synthesized live with the Web Audio API — there are no audio files, no song packs and nothing to install. The entire game is one web page that opens and plays instantly, even on modest hardware.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes — tap the three lanes directly, sized for thumbs, with the field scaling to any screen. Desktop players can use A/S/D or the arrow keys, and every button is reachable by keyboard.

What happens if I miss notes, and is it free?

Missing never ends the run: the melody note simply stays silent, the backing beat continues, and your final accuracy sets your grade. The game is free with no in-game ads, no account and no purchases — grades, ranks and streaks save on your device.
