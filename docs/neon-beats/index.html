Neon Beats

🎮 Tap/click the three lanes. Keyboard: A S D or arrow keys hit lanes, Space starts, P pauses, M mutes.

A free rhythm game where the chiptune is synthesized live from each day's date: tap the falling notes in three lanes and YOU play the melody — a brand-new Track of the Day for the whole planet, every day.

How to play

  1. Pick Today's Track — the song is generated from the UTC date, so every player in the world taps the exact same notes. Practice serves a fresh random seed anytime.
  2. Notes fall down three lanes in time with the synthesized chiptune; tap a lane (or press A/S/D, or the arrow keys) as each note reaches the glowing line.
  3. ±60ms is Perfect (+300), ±130ms is Good (+150); the melody only sounds when you hit it, so accurate tapping literally plays the song. Missing never fails the run — the track always finishes and your accuracy sets your grade.
  4. Chain 20 / 50 hits for ×2 / ×3 score multipliers, finish with S/A/B/C grades and accuracy ranks from Bronze to Legend, then share your score card and keep the 🔥 daily streak alive.

Frequently asked questions

What is Neon Beats?

A free browser rhythm game: tap the falling notes as the lanes scroll and you play the melody — every correct hit sounds the next note of the song, synthesized live with Web Audio.

What is Track of the Day?

Each day a new chiptune track is generated from the date seed — identical for players worldwide. Compare accuracy, climb from Bronze to Legend, and keep your daily streak alive.

Do I need to download songs or an app?

No. The music is synthesized live in your browser with the Web Audio API; there are no files, no accounts and nothing to install — the whole game loads instantly.

What if I miss notes?

Nothing breaks. The backing beat keeps playing, the song always finishes, and your accuracy decides your grade (S/A/B/C) and rank — perfect for rhythm beginners.
