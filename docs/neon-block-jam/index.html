Neon Block Jam

🎮 Tap / click to select, drag to slide. Keyboard: arrow keys to move cursor, Enter to confirm. U = undo, R = reset.

Slide neon blocks through matching color gates — no timer, no lives, just pure puzzle zen with unlimited undo.

How to play

  1. Tap a block to select it, then drag along its axis to slide — it snaps to the grid one cell at a time.
  2. Blocks can only exit through their matching color gate on the outer wall; different-colored blocks block the gate.
  3. Clear every block from the board to win. Finish at or under par moves for 3 stars, with unlimited free undo.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a timer in Neon Block Jam?

No — deliberately timer-free with unlimited free undo. The only score is a move count vs par for stars, so you can think as long as you want without anxiety.

How do the color gates work?

Each gate on the outer wall has a color. A block of the same color can pass through and vanish. Blocks of any other color are blocked by the gate wall — that's the puzzle: figure out the right order.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Touch-drag controls, responsive layout, works in any modern browser. No download, no sign-up, no ads interrupting your puzzle.

How do I get 3 stars on a level?

Finish at or under par moves. Tip: clear the blocks that block other blocks' gates first, and keep the center of the board open as long as possible.
