Neon Alchemy

🎮 Tap or click two element chips to combine. Keyboard: Tab + Enter works too.

Combine Water, Fire, Earth and Electric to discover 42 neon elements — from Steam to Cyberpunk. Handcrafted recipes, a daily global puzzle, zero AI, instant load.

How to play

  1. Tap two element chips to combine them — Water + Fire becomes Steam, and 38 handcrafted recipes lead from the basics to Cyberpunk.
  2. Every discovery unlocks new combinations and fills your collection wall — track your progress out of 42 elements.
  3. Take the daily puzzle: one handcrafted board per day, identical worldwide — reach the target element using only the limited pool.
  4. Your discoveries save automatically. Reset the collection any time for a fresh one-run challenge.

Frequently asked questions

What is Neon Alchemy?

A free browser crafting game: combine Water, Fire, Earth and Electric to discover 42 neon elements, from Steam to Cyberpunk. No download, no account, and it loads instantly on any device.

Is there a daily challenge?

Yes. One handcrafted puzzle board per day, identical for everyone worldwide, with a shareable result — Wordle-style. Boards rotate by date and limit which elements you can use.

Does it use AI like Infinite Craft?

No. All recipes are handcrafted and built-in, so it loads instantly, works on school or office networks, and no discovery is ever wrong or random.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your collection and daily completions save automatically in your browser via localStorage. You can reset the collection from inside the game for a fresh start.
