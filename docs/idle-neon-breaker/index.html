Idle Neon Breaker

🎮 Zero controls required — the game plays itself. Tap the shop cards to buy upgrades.

An idle brick breaker where numbered bricks merge 2048-style and double while you watch — balls bounce and break on their own, offline progress is unlimited, and there are no forced ads.

How to play

  1. Balls bounce by themselves and damage bricks — each hit lowers a brick's number; at 0 it shatters into coins.
  2. Every 20 seconds bricks merge 2048-style: equal numbers combine and double (2+2→4, 4+4→8).
  3. Spend coins in the shop: more balls, more damage per hit, faster balls, bigger coin payouts.
  4. Close the tab any time — your coins keep earning at full rate offline, with no cap. Come back and claim.

Frequently asked questions

What is Idle Neon Breaker?

A free browser idle game: balls bounce and break numbered bricks on their own; bricks merge 2048-style and double over time; you buy upgrades and watch numbers explode. It runs in any browser — no download, no account.

Does it earn while I'm away?

Yes. Offline progress is full-rate with no cap — close the tab, come back, claim everything at once. No 'come back every 2 hours' nonsense.

Are there forced ads or paywalls?

No forced ads, ever. No accounts, no paywalls; progress saves automatically in your browser via localStorage.

What's the goal?

Grow your highest brick: 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 and beyond. Keep firepower ahead of the merges and chase the record.
