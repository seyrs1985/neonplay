Neon Block Blast

🎮 Drag with mouse or touch. Keyboard: Tab to pick a shape, arrows to move, Enter to drop.

The classic block-blast puzzle, cleaned up: drag neon shapes onto an 8×8 board, clear lines, chase combos — no timer, no forced ads, plus a same-board daily challenge.

How to play

  1. Drag one of the three neon shapes onto the 8×8 board — no gravity, blocks stay exactly where you drop them.
  2. Fill a full row or column to clear it. Clearing several lines at once multiplies your score.
  3. Placement scores the shape's cell count; chains and multi-line clears stack combos fast.
  4. When none of the three shapes fit anywhere, the run ends — try the daily challenge for a same-board global run.

Frequently asked questions

Is Neon Block Blast free to play?

Yes — it runs entirely in your browser with no download, account or forced ads. No interstitials interrupt your run, ever.

How does the daily challenge work?

Every day all players get the same seeded shape sequence. Your daily best is stored per date, so you can compare against yesterday and chase a new record.

Is there a timer or gravity?

No. Blocks stay exactly where you drop them and nothing falls — the only pressure is the board filling up. It is a pure spatial-planning puzzle.

How is scoring calculated?

Placing a shape scores its cell count. Each cleared line adds 10 points, with multipliers for clearing several lines at once and a combo streak for consecutive clears.
