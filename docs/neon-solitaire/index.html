Neon Solitaire

🎮 Drag cards, or tap a card then its destination. Double-click/double-tap auto-plays to foundations. Keyboard: Tab/arrows move, Enter select/place, U undo, D draw, H hint, A auto-finish.

Classic Klondike solitaire in neon: drag or tap cards, double-click sends them home, undo anything — plus a daily challenge with the same deal for everyone and a 🔥 streak to keep alive.

How to play

  1. Move cards onto the seven tableau columns in descending order with alternating colors — red on black, black on red. Only kings land on empty columns.
  2. Build the four foundations up from Ace to King, one suit each. Double-click or double-tap a card to send it home automatically.
  3. Tap the stock to draw one card; when it runs out, tap again to recycle the waste and keep going. Every deal stays winnable through unlimited recycles.
  4. Finish fast for a time bonus and star rating; the Daily tab deals the same seeded board to every player worldwide and grows your 🔥 streak.

Frequently asked questions

Is this real Klondike solitaire?

Yes — seven tableau columns, four foundations built A to K by suit, descending alternating-color stacking, kings on empty columns, draw-one stock with unlimited recycles: the full classic ruleset, nothing simplified.

Is it free with no ads in the game?

Completely free: no interstitials, no energy timers, no account. Ads never interrupt a deal — your streak, best score and settings save locally in your browser.

What is the daily challenge and the 🔥 streak?

Every UTC day the Daily tab deals one seeded board identical for every player worldwide. Clear it and your 🔥 streak grows — miss a day and it resets, so come back tomorrow.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes — big touch targets, drag or tap-to-move (no precise dragging needed), double-tap auto-play, and the board scales to fit any phone screen without scrolling.
