Neon Tide

Ride the neon tide — a synthwave arcade shooter with procedural sound, waves of glowing enemies and boss pressure.

🎮 Mouse / touch to move, hold to shoot. Keyboard: arrow keys.

How to play

  1. Move with the mouse or arrow keys — your ship follows on the lower half of the screen.
  2. Hold to fire; dodging matters as much as shooting because enemies return fire in patterns.
  3. Survive escalating waves, chase the score multiplier, and beat your personal best.

Frequently asked questions

Is Neon Tide free?

Yes — it runs entirely in your browser for free, with no download, account or payment. It is an original game built by the NeonPlay team.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The game auto-scales to any screen and supports touch: drag to move your ship, it fires automatically while you hold.

Who made Neon Tide?

We did — it is an exclusive NeonPlay original, with every sound and effect generated in code (no asset files).

Does the game track me?

No. The game runs 100% locally in your browser; your scores never leave your device.
