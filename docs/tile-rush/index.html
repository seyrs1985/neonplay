Tile Rush

🎮 Tap / click tiles (top-most only). Keyboard: arrows move focus, Enter picks, 1 = Pull-out, 2 = Shuffle.

The sheep-game formula, minus the ads: tap stacked tiles into a 7-slot tray, match 3 identical to clear, and don't overflow — every deal is mathematically guaranteed solvable, with a daily board shared worldwide.

How to play

  1. Tap a tile that isn't buried — top-layer tiles glow cyan, buried ones are dimmed — and it flies into your 7-slot tray at the bottom.
  2. Collect 3 identical tiles in the tray and they burst away; clear every tile on the board to win the deal.
  3. If the tray fills all 7 slots without a match, the run is over — but the board stays the same, so retry the exact same deal and plan a better route.
  4. Each run grants one Pull-out (return up to 3 tray tiles to the board) and one Shuffle; score rewards speed and unused props.

Frequently asked questions

What is Tile Rush?

A free layered tile-matching puzzle: tap tiles into a 7-slot tray, match 3 identical to clear, and don't let the tray overflow. Every board is generated to be solvable — no dead deals.

Is there a daily puzzle?

Yes: a seeded board identical worldwide every day (the "same wind, same board" culture), track your streak with one free mulligan per month, and replay the same deal to beat your time.

Is it really free without ads?

Yes: no interstitials, no energy systems, no signup; unlimited play right in the browser, progress saved on your device.

What if I get stuck?

Each run includes one Pull-out (return up to 3 tray tiles to the board) and one Shuffle. Tip: keep the tray under 4 tiles and clear top layers first.
