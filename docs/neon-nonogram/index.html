Neon Nonogram

🎮 Tap cells to cycle fill / cross / clear. Keyboard: arrows + Space cycle, X marks a cross, Enter checks.

Use the row and column number clues to fill cells and reveal hidden neon pixel art — 12 solver-verified puzzles you can finish by pure logic, plus a daily board.

How to play

  1. Numbers beside each row and column tell you the runs of filled cells — '2 1' means a block of 2, then a gap, then a single.
  2. Tap a cell to fill it, tap again to mark a cross (definitely empty), tap again to clear.
  3. Fill every correct cell to reveal the hidden pixel art — wrong cells flash red on check and can be fixed.
  4. Solve puzzles to grow your gallery, and come back daily for the featured board — same picture for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

What is Neon Nonogram?

A free picture-logic puzzle: use the row and column number clues to fill cells and reveal hidden neon pixel art. No download, no account, plays in any browser.

Do I ever need to guess?

Never. All 12 puzzles are solver-verified to be solvable by pure logic, step by step — a fairness promise most nonogram apps can't make.

Is it really ad-free?

Yes. No interstitials, no energy systems, no paywalls; your gallery progress saves automatically in your browser.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes: big touch-friendly cells, three-tap marking (fill / cross / clear), full keyboard support on desktop, and a new daily picture every day.
