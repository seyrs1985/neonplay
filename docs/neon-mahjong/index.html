Neon Mahjong

🎮 Tap or click tiles. Keyboard: arrows move focus, PageUp/PageDown switch layer, Enter/Space select, H hint, N new deal, M mute.

Free mahjong solitaire with a guarantee: every board is verified clearable with zero reshuffles. Tap matching free tiles, clear the stack, and race the daily deal that's identical worldwide.

How to play

  1. A tile is free when no tile sits on top of it and at least one of its left or right neighbours is empty — free tiles glow, blocked tiles are dimmed.
  2. Tap two free tiles with the same face to remove them; chain matches within 4 seconds to grow the combo multiplier up to x2.
  3. Clear all tiles to win: 100 points per pair, a time bonus, and a +100 perfect-deal bonus if you never needed the reshuffle.
  4. The Daily tab deals one seeded board to every player worldwide (UTC) — your clear time sets your rank from 🟤 Bronze to 🏆 Legend and grows your 🔥 streak.

Frequently asked questions

What is Neon Mahjong?

A free mahjong solitaire game: remove matching tiles that are free — nothing stacked on top, one side open — until the board is clear. No download, no account, playable in your browser on any device.

Can I get a dead board?

No. Every deal is generated and then verified clearable without any reshuffle — the generator's clearing order is replayed and asserted before the board is dealt. An automatic reshuffle exists purely as a safety net and costs 100 points.

Is there a daily mahjong puzzle?

Yes — a seeded tile deal that is identical worldwide every UTC day, on the Wide layout. Your clear time earns a rank (Bronze to Legend), feeds your 🔥 streak with one monthly make-up card, and lands on your local top-10 leaderboard.

Is it senior friendly?

Yes: big 44px+ touch targets, high-contrast neon tiles with blocked tiles visibly dimmed, no timer pressure (the clock only tracks your own records), and full keyboard navigation for every action.
