Neon Typing

🎮 Desktop: just type A–Z; Space starts, P pauses, M mutes, Enter restarts. Touch: tap the input box to open the on-screen keyboard (simple mode with short words).

A typing speed test should take exactly as long as it promises and give you a number you can trust. Most free WPM tests are wrapped in sign-up walls, ad-heavy layouts or 3-minute marathons; the 60-second sprint is the format people actually want — long enough for accuracy to matter, short enough to retry immediately.

Neon Typing is that 60-second test with an arcade heart: your WPM (characters / 5 per minute) and accuracy update live while words drift toward a red line. Combos multiply your score, wrong keys break them, and breaching the line three times ends the run early — so speed and precision are scored together, not separately.

When the clock stops you get a finish card — WPM, accuracy, best combo, rank from Bronze to Legend — that copies as shareable text. The Daily Challenge hashes the UTC date into one word stream for the whole planet, and everything runs in your browser with no account and nothing to install.

Frequently asked questions

Is this typing speed test really free?

Yes — unlimited runs, no account, no download and no ads inside the game. Your best WPM and your last 20 runs are saved on your own device.

How long is the test?

Exactly 60 seconds, or sooner if three words cross the red line — the clock is the format, the line is the pressure. Practice mode gives you a fresh random stream every time.

What is a good WPM score?

An average typist lands around 40 WPM; 60 is fast, 80 is excellent and triple digits puts you in Legend rank territory. Your rank card makes the ladder easy to see.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — tap the input box and the on-screen keyboard opens in simple mode with short words. For the full experience a physical keyboard is king.
